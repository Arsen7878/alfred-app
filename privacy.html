Alfred — privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Alfred is a personal macOS application used only by its author, who is also its only user. This policy describes what Alfred does with data from the author's Google account.

Data accessed

With the gmail.readonly permission Alfred reads email messages (headers, text, names and sizes of attachments) when its owner searches or opens a message. It has no permission to send, delete or change email, and it does not download attachments or remote images.

Where data is kept

Messages are processed on the owner's Mac only. Alfred does not run any server and does not store email anywhere else. The Google access token is kept in the macOS Keychain on that Mac.

Sharing

Alfred does not sell, rent or share Google user data. The only exception is the owner's own choice to ask the built-in AI assistant about a message: then a filtered text excerpt — with email addresses, verification codes and full links removed — is sent to Anthropic's Claude under the owner's own subscription. Google user data is not used to train any model by Alfred.

Alfred's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Alfred at any time (this also revokes the token at Google), or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Via the author's GitHub profile: github.com/Arsen7878.
