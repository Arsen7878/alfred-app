Alfred

Alfred is a personal assistant application for macOS, written for and used only by its author. It is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone else.

What it does with Gmail

  • It requests one Google permission only: gmail.readonly — view email messages.
  • It never sends, deletes, moves or labels email, and never marks messages as read.
  • It does not open links, download attachments or load remote images from messages.
  • Email is shown on the owner's own Mac; nothing is stored on any server.
